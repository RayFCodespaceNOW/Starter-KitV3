Crew roles

Several areas of expertise are required.

A Mars crew would need piloting, scientific, engineering, and medical experience, as well as training for decisions made under pressure.

These profiles and names are fictional and do not represent actual astronauts or a selected crew.

Ground support

Surface crews depend on support from Earth.

Teams on Earth help plan activities, monitor the spacecraft, and troubleshoot problems. Communication delays mean crews also need procedures and training to make some decisions independently.
