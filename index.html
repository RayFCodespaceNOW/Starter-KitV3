Mission rationale

Why study Mars?

This fictional concept is based on scientific and engineering questions relevant to future human missions to Mars.

01 / SCIENCE

Study Martian geology

Examine rocks and terrain to investigate the planet's geological history and past environments.

02 / OPERATIONS

Evaluate surface work

Assess how crews could manage resources, use local materials, and maintain their health.

03 / COMMUNICATION

Share the results

Present findings and limitations in a form that students and the public can follow.

Surface operations

Working on Mars requires careful planning.

A thin atmosphere, radiation, dust, and extreme cold create demanding conditions. AURORA considers the systems and procedures a crew would need to operate on the Martian surface.
